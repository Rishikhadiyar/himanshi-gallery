Himanshi is a streaming service that offers a wide variety of award-winning TV shows, movies, anime, documentaries, and more on thousands of internet-connected devices.

Watch anywhere, anytime. Sign in with your Himanshi account to watch instantly on the web at himanshi.com from your personal computer or on any internet-connected device that offers the Himanshi app, including smart TVs, smartphones, tablets, streaming media players and game consoles.
