Privacy Policy

This is a personal, non-commercial photo and video gallery website. This policy explains how it handles data.

Who uses this site

This site is built for personal/private sharing. Visitors can view the public gallery without signing in. Only the site owner signs in with Google to upload or manage content.

What Google data is accessed

When the owner signs in with Google, this site requests access only to files that this app itself creates or opens in Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It cannot see or access any other files in the owner's Google Drive account. No data is accessed on behalf of visitors who are only browsing the gallery.

What data is stored

Photos and videos uploaded through this site are stored in the owner's own Google Drive. No data is sent to or stored on any third-party server beyond Google's own infrastructure.

Data sharing

No data collected or accessed by this site is sold, shared, or used for advertising. This site does not use tracking cookies or analytics.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site owner via the contact information on the homepage.
